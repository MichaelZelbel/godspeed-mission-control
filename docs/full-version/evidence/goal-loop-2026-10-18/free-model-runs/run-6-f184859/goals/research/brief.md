## The limit now
LIMIT: Lack of a clear central idea for the lighthouse keeper story.
- We have not yet settled on a specific concept or theme for the story.

- Without a central idea, the story may lack focus and impact.

## What works now
- Writing a short story is a good way to practice for writing a novel (Neil Gaiman).
- Starting in medias res can create immediate engagement (Writers Digest).
- Being economical with words, as if paying for each word, tightens the story (Neil Gaiman).
