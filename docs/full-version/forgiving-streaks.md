# Habit progress in Godspeed Mission Control

Every real completed day stays in your lifetime total, even after a break. A current streak counts
completed opportunities in the present run. Protected or excused days preserve that run without
adding completed days. Only your chosen weekdays are expected.

Open a habit on the Trackers shelf to see its small action, lifetime total, current run, next
milestone, and seven recent days. Each day has a weekday, date, and symbol with a written meaning.
Colour is an additional cue. The same labelled calendar can accompany a habit reply on Telegram.
The picture belongs to that reply; it does not create another reminder or post anywhere else.

## Missing days and returning

Two automatic protections are available per habit within a rolling 30-day period. Each used
protection becomes available again after 30 days. They cost nothing. A protected day adds no
activity to your total.

A day without an answer remains unknown for 48 hours after that local day ends. After that, an
available protection can preserve the recorded run. Without protection, the gap ends the recorded
run. It is labelled a gap in the record, rather than claiming you did not do the activity. An
explicit answer of “not done” remains distinct from an unanswered day.

After a break, your lifetime total and earned milestones remain. The next completion starts a new
run. You do not need to do extra work to return.

## Holidays, illness, and corrections

Use “Pauses and display” on the habit page to pause a habit or excuse an inclusive range of dates.
Illness, holidays, caring duties, and outages can be excused afterwards. These do not use scarce
protections. Existing “Does not count” answers remain unlimited exemptions.

Record a late completion on the actual day it happened. Correcting a date’s answer recalculates
the run and refunds any protection that date no longer needs. Repeating a completion on the same
date never adds another completed day. Undoing the latest answer can reveal an earlier answer
for that date, as the history explains.

The coach can also change a habit’s planned weekdays with an effective date. Earlier scheduled
opportunities keep the schedule that applied then. Habit dates use a fixed timezone with midnight
as the day boundary; travelling does not silently move earlier entries to another date.

## A quieter display

Choose the calm display to keep lifetime progress and the calendar while hiding streaks and
achievement milestones. There are no leaderboards, paid repairs, currencies, or countdowns.
Graduating a habit is an achievement that remains if you later resume it. Factual corrections can
change totals and any completion milestone that depended on an incorrect answer.

## Historical coverage and current limits

Existing habits retain every recorded completion. Older files may not say when a schedule changed
or when a pause began. Installation records their current schedule and timezone from the upgrade
date and explains that earlier streak history is incomplete. It never reconstructs a perfect old
streak from a schedule whose history is unknown.

This version supports daily habits and selected weekdays. Weekly quantity targets, such as three
sessions per week, need a different model and are not scored as weekly streaks. Talk attendance
streaks are deferred until records reliably distinguish participation from opening or closing a
conversation.

Lifetime totals use the whole habit record even when the visible history is shorter. The display
does not provide a durable offline queue: a change is confirmed only after the server saves it.
If a connection fails, record the activity later with its original date.
