Godspeed Mission Control v2, the first beta (2.0.0-beta.1), 19 October 2026.

This is the version for the first people who install and use it on their own. Updating keeps your folder, your notes and your settings.

Your goals have their own page in the notebook: the weekly number and its readings, today's decision, the steps and whether they worked, and what the research found. A number you tell the notebook or the chat now counts toward your goal's weekly number, and steps you allowed go out even without a morning brief. Coaching talks are prepared the night before, so a first goal now starts seven routines.

Connecting your AI in Settings now also connects your routines, and Settings says whether they can reach it. Settings also has an About section with your version and release code, and its notification switches really turn the morning brief and the weekly review on and off.

On a Mac the notebook starts when you log in and runs in the background. mc-uninstall removes the program from a Mac or Linux computer and leaves your mission control folder. Notes show their comments, nightly backups start on day one, and a failed backup is said in plain words.
