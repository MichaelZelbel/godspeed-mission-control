- Four parts: who it is (only the persona paragraph of AGENTS.md; a manual without the persona marks is offered the marks), what it may and may not do (`rules/*.md`, with the instructions for connected AI apps that My Profile held), what it knows about you (`profile/*.md`), how it does its jobs (`skills/*/SKILL.md`). Undo and restore are new commits; nothing is rewritten.
- A rule change rewrites the list in AGENTS.md in the same change, through `tools/compile-rules.js` (now callable as functions; an owner's own block keeps its opening line, closing paragraph and length). A change that would take AGENTS.md past 20,000 characters, or add words Hermes' injection scan would blank the whole manual for (`core/context-scan.mjs`, ported from Hermes' `tools/threat_patterns.py`), is refused in plain words. A file waiting in Review conflicting edits is not changed until it is settled. An edited packaged recipe stays the reader's on the next update (the existing `packaged-skills.mjs` policy).
- Hermes puts SOUL.md in its prompt first, as the identity, before AGENTS.md, and seeds it with "You are Hermes Agent, built by Nous Research ..."; routines read SOUL.md and skip AGENTS.md. SOUL.md now follows the persona paragraph (`core/assistant-soul.mjs`: at wiring, at every change, and when AGENTS.md changes on disk), with the replaced text kept beside it. A SOUL.md its owner wrote is left alone and named on the page, which offers to replace it (keeping a copy).
- The assistant changes the same files through MCP `identity_files`, only when the owner's words are found in a message they sent in the last 20 minutes (the notebook's chat records, or Hermes' own `state.db`), never with another program's key. Hermes' own file tools cannot write AGENTS.md or SOUL.md in the notebook's chat or Telegram (their approval gate fails closed there), so this is the only way "rename yourself" works in chat.
- Agent instructions stay records (`agent_instructions`), so connected apps keep receiving them through `get_user_profile`; the page lists, edits, pauses, removes and brings them back with their record history. My Profile's tab leads there. Converting them into `rules/` would take them away from the apps that read them and could break the rules list's ceiling.
