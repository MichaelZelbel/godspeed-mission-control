# your assistant can use a browser on your computer, for the sites where you are logged in
The kit for *Teach It Once*, updated 2026-10-02.

**What changed since v2.17.0**

- **Your assistant can use a browser on your computer.** Some jobs need your logins: your orders, your bank, your bookings. The installer now asks one question, and the box starts unticked: "Let your assistant use a browser on this computer?" With a yes, your assistant gets its own Chrome window on your computer, called Godspeed Chrome. You log in there once for each site it may use. Your everyday Chrome stays open and is never touched. If you have no Chrome, Edge is used instead.
- **It works with a Godspeed server in Docker.** Your computer opens one connection out to your server, so no port opens at home and there is nothing to set up on your router. The installer asks for a connection code: write to your assistant in Telegram "connect my computer" and paste what it sends. A code works once, for half an hour.
- **You stay in charge.** Your assistant asks you before it buys, sends, posts or deletes anything, and it never types your passwords: when a site needs a login, it opens the login page in Godspeed Chrome and asks you to log in. Pause it, resume it, see every page it opened, or stop lending your browser from Start > Godspeed Mission Control on Windows, Applications > Godspeed Mission Control on a Mac, or `godspeed-browser pause` in a terminal. Or tell your assistant "stop using my computer".
- **When your computer is off or asleep, your assistant says so.** One sentence, and the job runs by itself when your computer is back. A job that waits a whole day is dropped with one line.
- **Your home network stays private.** Godspeed Chrome never opens your router, printers or other devices at home, takes no downloads, and your assistant cannot read your logins out of it.
