# Optional context transfer to a notebook

The core import is `profile/bring-your-context-with-you.md`, used in Chapters 1 to 3. It produces a reviewable summary of accessible background, not every conversation or a complete account export.

If you also want selected material in an online notebook, complete Chapters 28 and 29 first. Review what may travel, choose its original home and avoid maintaining duplicate profile facts manually. The standard sync excludes `profile/` and `AGENTS.md`.

Use the service's actual export controls for a conversation export. Do not call an assistant-written summary an export. Keep credentials and other excluded material out of both.
