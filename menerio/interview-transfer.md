# Optional questions for missing notebook context

Use existing notes before asking for background again. The core route imports the reviewed Chapter 1 summary into four local profile files; no notebook interview is required.

If your optional notebook lacks an important fact, identify that specific gap. Confirm the fact and the intended original location before saving it. A notebook-only answer should say when a role or relationship exists only in the local profile, rather than infer it from a name.

For connection and visibility limits use `mcp-connection.md` and `the-notebook.md`.
