# Reader Test for Book Titles

Show three to five title-subtitle pairs in randomized order. Do not say which one the author prefers.

## Respondent fit

1. Briefly describe your role or situation.
2. How relevant is this book topic to you? 1 to 5

## Title questions

For each option:

1. What do you think this book will help you do, understand, or experience?
2. Who do you think it is written for?
3. How credible does the promise feel? 1 to 5
4. How distinctive does it feel compared with other books in the category? 1 to 5
5. What feels confusing, exaggerated, generic, or unattractive?

## Choice

1. Which option would you investigate first?
2. Why?
3. Which option would you be least likely to investigate?
4. Why?

## Delayed recall

After 30 minutes or the next day, without showing the list:

1. Which titles or phrases do you remember?
2. What did you think the remembered book promised?

## Analysis

Compare:

- Preference
- Correct comprehension
- Audience recognition
- Credibility
- Distinctiveness
- Delayed recall

Reject options that win votes but create the wrong expectation.
