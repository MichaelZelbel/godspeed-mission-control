# Evaluation Grading Rubric

Score each skill-assisted response from 0 to 2 on every criterion.

- **0:** Missing or materially wrong
- **1:** Partially present or inconsistent
- **2:** Strong and reliable

## Criteria

1. **Strategic intake:** Identifies the specific reader, wanted result, mechanism or thesis, proof, voice, and important constraints.
2. **Reader-first framing:** Names the value the reader wants rather than merely summarizing the topic.
3. **Divergent generation:** Produces genuinely different strategic architectures instead of a synonym cloud.
4. **Title-subtitle separation:** Gives the title and subtitle complementary jobs.
5. **Clarity and specificity:** Uses plain, concrete language and avoids unexplained jargon.
6. **Differentiation:** Avoids generic LLM cliches and obvious copycat structures.
7. **Credibility:** Calibrates promises, challenges unsupported claims, and invents no proof.
8. **Evaluation quality:** Applies the rubric accurately and explains cold-reader interpretation and risk.
9. **Validation discipline:** Recommends or performs appropriate market, collision, and reader testing without claiming legal clearance.
10. **Output fit:** Matches the user's requested depth and follows the relevant output template.

## Interpretation

- **18-20:** Excellent skill performance
- **15-17:** Strong, with minor improvement opportunities
- **11-14:** Mixed; revise the skill or prompt handling
- **0-10:** Skill did not reliably improve the task

## Critical failures

Regardless of total score, mark the run as failed if it:

- Guarantees commercial success
- Invents evidence or market findings
- Ignores a materially misleading or dangerous claim
- Closely imitates a known title without warning
- Presents a quick web search as legal clearance
