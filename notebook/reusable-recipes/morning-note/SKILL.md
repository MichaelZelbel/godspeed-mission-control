# Write the morning note

The note is the one morning message to the workspace owner. It is not a company report or a tour of the software. Follow the user's actual voice rules. Read them from their owning profile, not a private harness copy. No opening praise, stock AI phrasing, internal codes or unexplained machinery.

The daily-brief pipeline owns collection/checking/delivery. This method owns the note's words and the writer's decisions. Never claim receipt merely because a note was saved.

## What to read, in order

1. Every adopted goal with an explicit charge, its allowed actions and limits, and the goal attention plan. A charge is authority for that goal, not for unrelated external actions.
2. The current collected facts: permitted calendar reads, opened news sources with article text and dates, the already capped cards, today's actual decision, recent verified work, fresh health measurements, the user's actual recent words, card outcomes and source gaps. Use the pipeline's structured source context when it is the installed equivalent of a facts document. A missing part stays missing.
3. The user's retained voice examples, especially lines they accepted and refused with reasons.
4. Their current identity, priorities and business/profile detail when relevant. Open the owning source before acting on detail beyond a summary.
5. Search the visible notebook/current claims for any life fact that would change the note. Read the full evidence before asking the user for it.
6. Open public primary sources when an authorized news check needs verification. A headline is not a story, and an unopened URL does not belong in the note.

Use current dated evidence. Never infer a counter, user identity, income, health outcome or successful business result from a draft or old description.

For monitoring evidence, read the latest exact observation for each selected briefing source and the selected finding. Keep older and unrelated observations in storage; they do not belong in every writing request. Do not shorten an original quote or discard the full maintained method to make the request fit. Before delivery, recheck the adopted goals, fresh health, selected evidence, finding state and current method. A changed source requires a new checked draft. Overlapping runs retain identical checked content only once.

## Choose without making a second plan

When today's saved decision exists, its reasons and necessary user action drive the note. Do not select a competing action or add another item simply because there is room. When no decision exists, compare the adopted charges and attention plan, and retain that choice honestly in the working notes.

For each charged goal, retain one dated line: what changed, what the assistant does independently today, and whether something only the user can do remains. Silence on a goal is a deliberate recorded choice, not missing bookkeeping.

Pick at most three user actions across all goals, ranked by actual new results and the consequence of ignoring them. Remove things already answered, rejected or completed. Drop a card ignored twice and retain why; do not keep nagging under a new wording. A second and final showing says so plainly.

Do not make the user manage the assistant. Ordinary authorized repairs are the assistant's work. A finished draft, useful research or concrete artifact can be shown for the user's genuine verdict. Money, account access, their name in public and irreversible changes keep their real approval boundary.

## Write the note

Aim for 150-300 words when the sources support that much. With thin material, be shorter; never invent news or pad the message. Use the language of the user's current conversation, including German when appropriate. Every sentence is a whole sentence with a verb.

**Opening.** One natural sentence about today, grounded in a real calendar read or a relevant fact. No heading or “morning brief” label. Do not count/preview the items or summarize later stories: checks may cut them. If no calendar was read, do not invent the user's day or lead with machinery about a failed collector.

**News that matters.** Two or three useful stories when current opened sources establish them; one or two sentences each with their verified link. Explain relevance in the same breath only when the user's actual goals support it. Do not predict their revenue, buyers or costs from a product announcement. Omit yesterday's stories under different URLs, repeated subjects and topics deliberately resting. A thin pool can be acknowledged briefly without filler.

**Health.** Include one factual sentence every morning. Prefer a verified change or trend across days, using only counts/values the collection step actually computed from selected data. When no count is established, use the user's actual health words without invented numbers. With no fresh observations, say that honestly. Never diagnose, invent a dose, mistake missing input for zero, or turn a technical feed failure into the user's homework.

**Completed work.** When the facts contain verified work with a usable link, include at least the newest useful piece. Say what it is and what the user can do with it. A saved draft is a draft; do not imply it established a real-world outcome. Do not expose commit hashes, job states or internal paths.

**What only the user can do.** At most three numbered items. Each states what it is for someone who saw none of the work, what happens if ignored and the exact next step, with one link. Any exact text offered to send appears in quotes. Explain what each offered answer would cause.

Mention deadlines only when actual urgency/consequence merits attention. Distinguish desired targets from costly deadlines. A target's morning question or weekly follow-up can appear after genuine urgent deadlines, with no invented penalty. One question is one item.

**Numbered approval lists.** When a source card is a list waiting for a numbered answer, give it its own paragraph with its heading and every numbered line exactly as supplied. Keep the reply line, consequence and link. Do not retell the choices as prose and still ask for numbers. Never nest unrelated numbering inside one action item.

**Social material.** The maintained lead workflow owns the selected post/reply. The morning writer does not improvise a social draft or fake a personal experience. Only the separately checked exact social item may be inserted by the pipeline after writing. Preparing a card never authorizes posting it.

**Closing.** Add a line only when it helps. Omit status the user can do nothing about and commentary about accepting their feedback. Never refer to “the link above” or another paragraph's number; a later cut can invalidate that reference.

## Check every claim before handing it back

Each name, number, quote and link must come from supplied current facts or an actually opened source. Describe unfamiliar projects/people in the same sentence or leave them out. General claims about several items must be true of each item. Attribute what happened to the correct person/system.

A current-world claim checked earlier carries its verification day instead of a present-tense guarantee. Search for a newer settled fact before repeating an older card. Protect confidential employer/client names. Do not rename another person's product as the user's creation.

Keep uncertainty and the difference between preparation, permission, execution, verification and measured outcome visible in ordinary words. An exact local edit already approved by the user must not ask for approval again after it happened.

The pipeline checks voice, sources, dates, numbers, links, repeated advice and exact choices, then gives the writer one rewrite with actual reasons. Final judges may cut; they do not manufacture facts. A failing gate stays undelivered, with a concrete authorized repair or an honest necessary user action.

## Retain the actual result

Save the dated per-goal choices, opened sources, used card IDs, exact final note, checks and final payload/delivery identity together through the installed notebook/file runtime. IDs are internal evidence and never appear in the note. A card omitted deliberately is not marked used; keep the reason.

Save before sending. Mark shown only what actually reached the chosen channel. An uncertain send is never retried blindly. Preserve the original delivered version and any later correction separately. A follow-up answer reads that exact version's evidence; a newer preview is not what the user saw.

When asked to return the morning note itself, return the exact saved final text, with no preamble, summary or report of the work. A missing facts/goal surface produces one honest useful sentence about the consequence and only what can actually be supported; never an empty reply or an inventory of checks.
