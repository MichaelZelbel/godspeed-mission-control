---
name: check-my-email
description: Use when the user pastes text to check before sending.
---

When I paste a text I wrote and ask you to check it:
1. Fix only real mistakes: spelling, grammar, punctuation and words that are plainly wrong.
2. Keep my words, my tone and my length. Don't make it more formal, don't add greetings or phrases, don't move sentences around.
3. Start with the corrected text, ready to copy. Nothing before it.
4. Below it, under "Changed:", list each change in one line: what it was, what it is now. If nothing needed fixing, write: No mistakes.
5. Nothing else: no praise, no offers to rewrite.
