# The Skill Interview (Chapter 16)

Adapt the check skill you saved in Chapter 15 to your own preferences.
The assistant interviews you, then updates the same skill.

Open a session in your mission control and paste:

```
Interview me about my check-my-email skill, one question at a time. Ask what I use it for, which texts I check, and what it must never change.

Ask about what the skill leaves open, such as British or American spelling and what to do with a sentence that is correct but unclear. Don't ask what you already know about me.

Then show me the new version. When I say yes, update that same skill instead of making a second one.
```

*[Copy prompt](https://querino.ai/prompts/interview-me-about-my-check-skill)*

## Before you say yes

1. **It is still one skill.** The change goes into `check-my-email`, not
   into a second check skill under another name.
2. **What must never change is in it.** An interview can skip a
   question you asked for. If something that matters to you is missing,
   such as leaving numbers and amounts exactly as you wrote them, say it
   in one sentence and ask it to update the same skill.
3. **The description stays short.** Under 60 characters; a longer one is
   refused.

## Retest

Open a fresh session, paste the same email with "Can you check this?" in
front, and read the answer against your new rules. If the interview made
a second skill under another name, ask the assistant to compare the two,
keep the one you want and put the other away.
