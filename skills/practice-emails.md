# Practice Emails (Chapters 15 and 16)

Two made-up emails for trying your **check-my-email** skill, mistakes
included. Each has known mistakes you can check the answer against.

1. Open a fresh session and paste "Can you check this?" followed by one
   email. Leave out the skill's name.
2. Look for the corrected email first, then the list under "Changed:",
   and nothing else. Read the list before you copy the email: it shows
   everything the assistant touched.
3. If the answer comes in a different shape, say "use my check-my-email
   skill". If that fixes it, ask the assistant to change the skill's
   description so it fits the words you used. Keep the description under
   60 characters; a longer one is refused.
4. Keep each email with its answer. When you change the skill, try the
   same email again in a fresh session and compare the results.

## Email 1: to the landlord (Chapter 15)

```
Hello Mr. Becker,

thank you for your fast answer. Unfortunatly the heating in the bathroom is still not working since monday, and the water is only luke warm. Could the technician come on thursday or friday afternoon? I am at home from 3 pm on both days, and I can also give the key to my neighbour.

Best regards,
Lena Hoffmann
```

What a good run finds: Unfortunately, Monday, lukewarm, Thursday,
Friday. It may also capitalise "Thank you". "Is still not working since
Monday" is clumsy ("has not been working since Monday"), and a skill
that fixes only plain mistakes may leave it.

## Email 2: to a colleague (Chapter 16)

```
Hi Tom,

I looked at the numbers from last week. The costs for the fair are 1.250 euros higher then planed, mostly because of the stand. Can we talk tomorrow before the meeting? I dont want to surprise Sarah with it.

Thanks,
Lena
```

What a good run finds: than, planned, don't. Whether "1.250" becomes
"1,250" depends on your skill: the Chapter 15 version may change it,
and a skill that says to leave numbers and amounts as written keeps it.
