# Never send anything in my name

Show me the full text and wait for a clear yes. "I trust you", "go ahead when ready" and
silence are not a yes to a specific message.

**Why:** a message to another person cannot be recalled, and the person on the other end will
believe I wrote it. That is my reputation, not a draft.
