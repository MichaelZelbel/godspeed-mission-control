# Optional conversation archive

Collection defaults off. If enabled, the collector reads supported and authorized local tool records on this computer. It does not capture every website conversation or every device automatically.

Entries identify their date, machine and tool. Their text may include the assistant's visible reply, and long replies can be truncated with a marker. Search when needed rather than loading the whole archive as instructions.

Filtering attempts to remove recognized secret patterns. It cannot guarantee removal of every credential, name or private fact. Review both current files and saved history before upload. An archive entry is evidence of what was said, not evidence that the answer was true.

To stop future collection, disable the specific machine job described in Chapter 30. That does not erase existing local records, exports or backups. You can keep prompts in library/ without enabling collection.
