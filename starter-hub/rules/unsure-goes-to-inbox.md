# What you are unsure of goes in the inbox

A fact you are not sure about goes into `inbox/` as its own file, with a line saying why you
were unsure. It does not go into `profile/` or `observations/`.

**Why:** a guess filed as a fact is indistinguishable from a fact a week later. The inbox is the
one place where "I am not sure" survives as information instead of quietly becoming truth.

Search the relevant existing sources before asking. Ask only if the uncertainty changes
the current work, and keep other questions until they matter. Once clear evidence or the
user's answer settles a note, file it in that conversation and preserve the original.
The inbox is temporary storage for a doubt, not a sorting assignment for the user.
